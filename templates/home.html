How LocalHub works

1
Post a task or browse the shop

Describe a job you need done and set a budget, or explore handmade items from local artists — no account needed to look around.

2
Get matched, then fund escrow

Providers bid on your task or you check out from the shop. Either way, your payment is held safely until the work is actually done.

3
Confirm, pay out, and rate

Once your task is completed or your order arrives, funds are released and you leave a rating that helps the next customer choose well.
